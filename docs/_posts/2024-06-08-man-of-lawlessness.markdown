<bref>Rev 13:11-18 ESV</bref>
.<bref>11</bref> Then I saw another beast rising out of the earth. It had two horns like a lamb and it spoke like a dragon. <bref>12</bref> It exercises all the authority of the first beast in its presence, and makes the earth and its inhabitants worship the first beast, whose mortal wound was healed. <bref>13</bref> It performs great signs, even making fire come down from heaven to earth in front of people, <bref>14</bref> and by the signs that it is allowed to work in the presence of the beast it deceives those who dwell on earth, telling them to make an image for the beast that was wounded by the sword and yet lived. <bref>15</bref> And it was allowed to give breath to the image of the beast, so that the image of the beast might even speak and might cause those who would not worship the image of the beast to be slain. <bref>16</bref> Also it causes all, both small and great, both rich and poor, both free and slave, to be marked on the right hand or the forehead, <bref>17</bref> so that no one can buy or sell unless he has the mark, that is, the name of the beast or the number of its name. <bref>18</bref> This calls for wisdom: let the one who has understanding calculate the number of the beast, for it is the number of a man, and his number is 666.


What or who are the beast? The 2nd letter of Peter gives the answer.
<bref>2 Pet 2:12,13</bref>
.<bref>12</bref> But these, like irrational animals, creatures of instinct, born to be caught and destroyed, blaspheming about matters of which they are ignorant, will also be destroyed in their destruction, <bref>13</bref> suffering wrong as the wage for their wrongdoing. They count it pleasure to revel in the daytime. They are blots and blemishes, reveling in their deceptions, while they feast with you.

<bref>2 Pet 2:19</bref>
.<bref>18</bref> For, speaking loud boasts of folly, they entice by sensual passions of the flesh those who are barely escaping from those who live in error. <bref>19</bref> They promise them freedom, but they themselves are slaves of corruption. For whatever overcomes a person, to that he is enslaved. <bref>20</bref> For if, after they have escaped the defilements of the world through the knowledge of our Lord and Savior Jesus Christ, they are again entangled in them and overcome, the last state has become worse for them than the first.

The beast are people who have forsaken self-control and pursued evil desires for the sake of pleasure. By doing so, their mind has become debased. Their discernment is reduced to animal instincts rather than something in the range of nobility. Apparently, the worst offenders may be former Christ followers. After reading 2nd Peter slowly and thinking about church history, it seems Peter is prophesising about the Popes who were to follow him in the centuries after his death. Although, the beastly class of people is not revealed. Is Peter referring to Popes or to peasants? Are the beastly ones low-brow or high-brow people? Are they drunk with wine or drunk with power? Some beastly people are corrupted by a control instinct, others by a booze and sex instinct, others still by racial predjudice. What I mean is, it's not the low-brow racist hooligan that is the main concern for the future of society. Rather it is the one-in-a-million brilliant person who wants to rule and reign with absolute autonomy by his own scheme of nobility.

The beast is the hands, feet and mind of Satan just as the body-of-Christ is the hands, feet and mind of Jesus Christ. Notice that the false prophet tells 'the people' to create an image for the first beast who was wounded by the sword yet lived. The deception strategy of Satan will be the same it was in the beginning. That is, create a counterfeit of God's kingdom so that people are lead astray to worship false gods. The first beast could have a religious identity, because it suffered a mortal wound but was healed. A false Christ? Notice that the center of attention starts with the beast whose wound was healed, then moves to the false prophet. However, the false prophet seems extremely devious, because he directs attention towards the first beast, commanding people to worship him. The false prophet performs miracles always 'in the presence' of the first beast. Again, this sounds like a religious term, because the power of God for miracles demands the presence of God. The Saints of Jesus all know this : the breath of God <sup><a href="https://tehomic.github.io/blog/bible/exposition/2024/06/08/man-of-lawlessness.html#derekprince">[1]</a></sup> must accompany the word of God for authentic miracles to happen. That is, the Holy Spirit goes forth in the breath of a Jesus disciple when he/she proclaims the Word of God.

At this stage things get even more diabolical. The false prophet calls fire down from heaven which is the sign of an 'authentic' prophet. A sign that only the holy prophets of the old testament are known for. Again, it does the sign in the presence of the beast. Why in the presence of the beast? To promote the beast as a god-like ambassador of authority and power which is given to the false prophet. That's why it's more likely that the beast is a recognised benevolent society who **supposedly** seek to influence people for good and not for evil. It's not likely that the beast is initially opposed to the law or is linked to a crime-syndicate. Why? Because it's most likely that a religious and political scheme will be the platform that deceives the population majority. When considering religion, realise that the fundamental premise of religion could shift dramatically in the future because of technology. 

That's an introduction for the next evil character in this tale of Revelation. The false prophet commands 'the people' to create an image for the beast who was wounded yet lives. The people obediently make an image which is surely an autonomous android made in the image of man. That is, it has two arms, two legs, two eyes and a mouth. Nothing strange about that. This is a very real probability. There is actually a fairly obvious business reason to invest in an autonomous robot. The car industry use factory robots and have done for years. But a robot that can make decisions and act like a human is a huge leap forward for the world of business. As you can imagine, artificial intelligence developers are trying to achieve this right now. 

<bref>Rev 13:15 KJV</bref>
And he (false prophet) had power to give life unto the image of the beast, that the image of the beast should both speak, and cause that as many as would not worship the image of the beast should be killed.

<bref>Rev 13:15 NKJV</bref>
He (false prophet) was granted power to give breath to the image of the beast, that the image of the beast should both speak and cause as many as would not worship the image of the beast to be killed.

Notice that the image of the beast should **both** speak **and** cause people to be killed. The false prophet and the beast are not laying hands on anyone. The image, the synthetic man is commanding people to worship him or die. This surely is the man of lawlessness, who will sit in the temple at Jerusalem, claiming to be God. You might say "He can't be the man of lawlessness because he's not born of a woman". Well, as far as I know, the bible does not demand that he must be born of a woman. Again, you might say "This is all science fiction and a lot of nonsense". The Word of God says that the false prophet is allowed to "give breath" to the image. There is only one other reference in the bible that describes a Creator who gives breath to his creature. We understand that when the Lord God breathed life into man, the man became a living soul, which means the man became self-conscious and God-conscious. 

There are two attributes given to the image. One is the power to speak like a human, the other is to think with a human perception of spiritual power. The robot has become self-conscious, but without any reason to regard the Lord God of Israel, creator of heaven and earth. It has absolutely no fear of God, which is expected and not at all surprising. After all it is a synthetic man and not a human being. After all, the false prophet is the father of it's identity and it's alligence to Satan. This picture is very dark and ominous, but the Lord Jesus always has a plan to counter the evil schemes of Satan.

The Saints could pray for holy wisdom in order to build such an android in advance of the darkness that will be planning the same thing. However, the power to speak is by synthetic intelligence only and not by self-consciousness. The saints create the android for one reason. To demonstrate that it can be done and to warn the whole world that a man of lawlessness and the mark of the beast is coming and there is nothing that can be done to stop it from happening, because it is prophesised by the Word of God. After delivering the message and warning to the world, the saintly android machine would be shutdown and disassembled or perhaps converted to provide some useful good purpose.

About the number of the beast. It probably refers to the image of the beast, the synthetic man. It might be the hardware chassis number or the software version number of the robotic model. However, considering that the scipture says 'let the one who has understanding calculate the number of the beast', I don't think the value is meaningful unless it is calculated by discernment from Holy Spirit.

Perhaps the only way to avoid the mark of the beast is to escape planet earth and find a new earth to start again without the plague of spiritual death to deal with. Wonder what the Lord God of Israel would say to such an idea! My unction is this: He has responded with an Amen! By escaping this earth and migrating to another earth-like planet does not mean the second-coming of Jesus is nullified. The concept of Earth may not be limited to this planet. Rather, it just means that Earth is a type of planet that supports human life. This home planet earth is just the first one discovered! You might say "Now you're really talking science fiction and nonsense!". It is indeed science fiction in today's perception of possible. But nothing is impossible for those having the spirit and testimony of Jesus Christ! The Lord is already revealing true science ideas like virtual mass density and harmonic gravity. See related articles for more information. Think about the magnitude of glory that the Trinity receive when the Saints rise up and defeat Satan by escaping this domain of darkness and migrating away to a place where Satan can't come in to steal, kill and destroy. Here is a little scripture to support this idea ->

<bref>Matt 24:29-31 ESV</bref>
.<bref>29</bref> Immediately after the tribulation of those days the sun will be darkened, and the moon will not give its light, and the stars will fall from heaven, and the powers of the heavens will be shaken. <bref>30</bref> Then will appear in heaven the sign of the Son of Man, and then all the tribes of the earth will mourn, and they will see the Son of Man coming on the clouds of heaven with power and great glory. <bref>31</bref> And he will send out his angels with a loud trumpet call, and they will gather his elect from the four winds, from **one end of heaven to the other**.

The saints are gathered from one end of heaven to the other. This could be referring to saints who have died in the Lord, and gone to heaven. However, in verse 30 it says 'all the tribes of the earth will mourn'. This implies that the gathering activity is happening in the natural domain as well as heaven. We already know this from standard bible study. But perhaps 'heavenly gathering' means from one end of the universe to the other.

Other scriptures that gives some light on the subject is ->

<bref>Rev 12:14 ESV</bref>
.<bref>14</bref> But the woman was given the two wings of the great eagle so that she might fly from the serpent into the **wilderness**, to the place where she is to be nourished for a time, and times, and half a time.

With a crown of twelve stars the woman is Israel, and giving birth to a child is referring to the death, burial and resurrection of Jesus Christ, who is the **first born** from the dead.

Notice that this "light" is not natural light, but rather the light of life support. The clockwork of gravty is the light of life, because without it life would not exist on the earth. Consider that the proximity of the earth from the sun is called **perfect** by NASA on their <a target="_blank" href="https://science.nasa.gov/learn/heat/big-ideas/big-idea-2-1/"><u>Heliophysics</u></a> web page.

This paragraph discusses the proximity of earth from the sun, which NASA calls perfect. What defines Earth as a habitable planet in terms of placement? This is the definition : the Earth is located in space lying between two solar distance limits. The inner boundary is the hot limit the outer boundary is the cold limit. This is an amazingly accurate biblical testimony! What is more amazing is that the biblical usage describes remote habitable places or countries which are foreign compared to the local region. This biblical evidence is endorsing the idea that biblical Earth is not really referring to just our home planet, but rather it refers to any planet that can support human life, having the similar features compared to our earth. Such as approximately 70% water, mountains and valleys, salty oceans but fresh water rivers and lakes and the same proximity to a similar sized sun.
