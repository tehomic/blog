These verses support the original text in Genesis :

<bref>Gen 1:9-10 KJV</bref>
.<bref>9</bref> And God said, Let the waters under the heaven be gathered together unto one place, and let the dry *land* appear: and it was so <bref>10</bref> And God called the dry *land* Earth; and the gathering together of the waters called he Seas: and God saw that *it was* good

It seems there must be a connection between "waters gathered together unto one place" and "stretching out the heavens". Notice that the term "Seas" is plural. So the picture is a multitude of seas spread out and separated by non-water voids but still connected to each other. Like the Pacific and Atlantic oceans that are connected at Cape Horn, the southern tip of Africa. 

The earth also is "spread out" in the heavens. Another journal called "The Workdays of Creation" suggests that the Earth, our earth, may not really be the main attraction of Genesis. The term Earth is possibly not referring directly to our planet, but rather a general term, referring to collected star dust, the material dirt of planet composition throughout the universe. In the same way, the "gathering of waters" is not referring directly to the oceans and seas of our earth. 

This term is far more significant, it points to the progressive dispersal of worker angels who are assigned into groups, dwelling in the heavenly host where they are stationed. This is a firmament design function. Now the phase of creation has advanced and now the firmament is being filled and stretched.

The Firmament is a virtual universe populated by worker angels created to declare God's Word for generating the natural universe. Worker angels also govern the natural universe upholding God's laws for sustaining life. 

Notice that, in this part the natural universe is not even directly mentioned. The narrative is spiritual, describing a higher order creation, the building of the Lord God's kingdom. This is an important implication to grasp. Why is the spiritual account even mentioned? Imagine instead if the Lord God described the original natural creation in great detail. The whole world would be astonished and compelled to believe in the Lord God of Israel. Why? Because the spirtual account provides insight for living and walking by faith in God. The invisible government of Jesus Christ makes the visible appear. Hebrews says "By faith we know that the universe was made by the word of God, so that what is seen was made out of what is unseen". 

So what is the natural universe doing while "gathering of the waters" is happening? After worker angels are stationed in a host, they declare the creative commands of God. The picture is a super flood of hot matter and energy, dispersing from a single source, similar to the common "big bang" story. The gathering of angels into a host, corresponds to the synthesis of stars by separation of the hot material flood into smaller seas which are hydrogen gas collections that become the stars. Holy Spirit does the creative work, generating stars, planets and life on Earth.

If the story is about angels, why does the bible use the term waters? A worker angel has custody of God's creative commands, which is called water. He is defined by God's commands, the water that is entrusted to him. What an amazing use of language! It describes both the creation of the creators, and the intelligent design documented by a sea of commands. This is God's brilliant design principle : first document the design, the engineering commands of the Lord God, so that an account of what to do and when to do it is recorded. The worker angels are the living type of the creature that they produce, including stars and planets, mountains and trees.
