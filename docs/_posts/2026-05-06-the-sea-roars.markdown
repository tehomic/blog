The bible, both new and old testaments, describes two mysterious creatures called the Flood and the Sea which apparently is not a literal flood or sea or ocean. 

My unction says these are the same creature. The two names Flood and Sea describe two different powers that can effect the life and times of humanity. The Flood is a directed torrent of water that carries one or more people from one place to another. The Sea is a pervasive controlling and spiritual influence which is the heavenly hegemony of both light and dark angelic principalities.

<a target="_blank" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hegemony">Hegemony</a> refers to the dominant influence, authority, or leadership exerted by one group, state, or culture over others. It involves a mix of coercion (military/economic power) and consent (cultural/ideological influence), often making the dominant group's values seem like common sense. It is commonly used in international relations and social theory.

The Flood and Sea are creatures because they are created beings that have the power of speech because scripture says the Flood and Sea have a voice. This is not poetry.

Does the bible show the Flood in action, carrying people from one place to another? Yes, Dan 8:5 tells that the Greek army was carried by the Flood into battle with Persia. How does it say that? The feet of the goat which represents the Greek army did not touch the ground. In a natural sense, this happens when people are carried by flood waters while treading water. Notice psalm 18 says the channels of the sea were exposed which are a feature of the foundations of the world. How are the channels used? The channels of the Sea are traffic routes to move people from one place to another. For example, consider the ancient trade canals of Britain. Sea channels may not always be a sea floor structure, they also could be created by strong sea currents.

The heavenly Seas, are the virtual Sea of humanity and the virtual source of Man's intelligence. Not intelligence that is cognitive power but intelligent information that gifts Man with ideas and inspiration. Consider the story of Ahab the evil king of Israel and his demise. The false prophets encouraged Ahab to proceed and claim victory, but Micaiah the true prophet warned him that his prophets were virtually lying. This is indeed the case because the prophets had no idea they were lying by virtue of the angel imposing words in their mind. Since they were false prophets, they did not have a true witness about what they said. They were evil imposters, having no desire to even serve Ahab let alone the Lord God of Israel.

Another example is found in Daniel's life. King Nebucadnezer had a forboding dream which troubled him. He demanded the wise men tell him his dream before giving an interpretation, so that he would be sure they were truely wise and their counsel is trustworthy. The Chaldean wise men replied "This request is not reasonable since the answer belongs to the gods who do not dwell with men". The king, enraged and furious, commanded all the wise men be executed. Daniel using discretion won favor to have extra time to seek the Lord God and pray for grace. In truth, the Lord God gave Daniel intelligence about the king's dream.

Daniel forthrightly says "No wise man can reveal the mystery. Only the Lord God in Heaven who reveals mysteries". So Daniel humbly testified that the Flood reveals mysteries not a man with his intelligence. Can a person produce a "good idea" purely from their strong desire and intelligence? Yes, but it depends on the degree of power that the idea carries. I suggest that all world shaping "advancement" of society and technology does not ever spring from the pure intelligence of humanity. The early church is a good example of how the power of leadership and discretion is conducted from the head to the body. About the apostles in Corinth, Paul says "Who is Paul and who is Apollos? Only servants of Jesus doing his work. I planted the seed Apollos watered, but God causes the growth". Jesus is the head and heart of the Body, and He is in heaven. Good godly ideas that shape society are conceived in the heart of Christ then conducted through the head to the body. The Head sends one or more commands to the executive brain which stimulates the body to do work that gives glory to God.

What about the advancement of society which is not from God. There are many inventions that are not good. What about the internet, personal computer and smart phone? What about plastic? There are good applications hosted on the internet, there are good applications for the pc and smart phone, but there are lots of evil things conducted through them. Consider social media and how it can be a tool for nasty things like cyber bullying. The pinnacle of that computer tech is artificial intelligence, which is potentially a great evil. Having said that AI does some good in providing good value text composition for a requested subject. The threat of AI is quite profound. The potential goal of AI development is to produce a synthetic man who can follow programmed commands. That would be a great win for business according to the culture of Babylon. 

In a prophetic way, that might also fullfill prophecies about the Beast and it's image. The Beast is the hands and feet of Satan just as the Body of Christ is His hands and feet. Daniel chapter 7 describes a terrible beast that has iron teeth and is charged to devour much flesh. That flesh is not natural flesh, but spiritual, which is an arrangement provided by a persons Lord. The arrangement is a body, an authorized place with facilities that enables work and produces fruit to give glory to your Lord. The Lord of a person is not the one professed but actually the one obeyed. Let them hear who have ears to hear. A similar picture is described in Revelation, but there the scripture also tells of an image that is made by the people of the Beast to honor the Beast. Revelation says that the false prophet gives breath to the image so that it could speak. What is an image that can speak? Some think it might be something like a large bronze human statue that is given a mouth by magic arts. A better idea is a synthetic man, an android. This evokes the last days and a tribulation episode. An android that has two eyes, two ears and a mouth made in the image of man. This resonates with the creation of Man in the image of God. The android is a new version of man and suggestively a better version of man. But it is really a counterfeit man. The false prophet gives it intelligence to speak and is the most terrible kind of image. It may even be the incarnation of that man of lawlessness described by Daniel, and 2nd Thessalonians. 

This speaking image is diabolical because it has absolutely no fear of God. It is a synthetic man and has no spirit that could ascribe allegiance to God. This new man is probably faster, stronger, smarter and more noble than a natural man. I say more noble, because that might be how the secret power of lawlessness works. See 2 Thess 2:7. That is, it is more subtle and deceptive than all other deception on record. For example, the synthetic man may apparently even defy the programming rules of the beast and false prophet and initially proclaim allegiance to the Lord God. This man of intrigue may build for education, healing and charity. It may support the weak and vulnerable. But of course the purpose for all these good works is to steal the hearts of humanity so that devotion and worship to the image is only a heatbeat away. The story of Absalom is similar. He stole the hearts of Israel in order to steal their allegiance. Wise men and women may sound a warning about the subterfuge, but as Revelation says the image will cut them off. In the end, it would not be a surprise at all for this synthetic man to rebuild the temple in Jerusalem in order to sit in the temple of God claiming to be God. Revelation says that the image will demand that people worship it and will kill those who refuse.
