<bref>Luke 12:27 ESV</bref>
.<bref>27</bref>"Consider the lilies, how they grow: they neither toil nor spin, yet I tell you, even Solomon in all his glory was not arrayed like one of these".

Jesus says "consider the lillies, they neither toil nor spin, yet not even Solomon in all his glory was arrayed like one of these". Jesus is comparing the glory of Solomon, with the glory of the Lilly. In his early life, Solomon was a righteous king and the Lord God himself glorified Solomon's throne to magnify Israel and make her beautiful. His royal clothing was probably woven with gold threads. Yet Jesus says the glory of the Lilly surpasses the glory of Solomon. Specifically, the 'clothing' of the Lilly surpasses the royal spendor of Solomon. So what is clothing of the Lilly? It must be the flower, because that is the part that is designed to be attractive. 

Over the centuries, naturalists have studied the symbiotic relations between plants and the animals that pollinate
them. Why call it clothing though? Because in terms of making the plant noticable and desirable, it identifies the plant. Why do florists always use flowering plants? Because they look and smell attractive. There is another important feature of this comparison. The royal clothing of Solomon is natural glory, but the clothing of the Lilly is living glory. The flower is living clothing. It is a special member of the plant, and carries its reproductive parts. So Jesus is declaring that God desires to clothe his people with glory, according to the Holy Spirit. Why? To propogate the church and make it multiply.

<bref>Isaiah 61:10-11 ESV</bref>
.<bref>10</bref> I will greatly rejoice in the LORD; my soul shall exult in my God, for he has clothed me with the garments of salvation; he has covered me with the robe of righteousness, as a bridegroom decks himself like a priest with a beautiful headdress, and as a bride adorns herself with her jewels. <bref>11</bref> For as the earth brings forth its sprouts, and as a garden causes what is sown in it to sprout up, so the Lord GOD will cause righteousness and praise to sprout up before all the nations.

The 61st chapter of Isaiah is messianic and begins by declaring that Christ is annointed to 'preach the gospel to the poor' and 'bestow on the poor a crown of beauty instead of ashes'. Verse 10 says that the children of God are clothed with a robe of righteousness. Then verse 11 says that Holy Spirit will cause righteousness to multiply like the growth of a garden. The robe is 'living clothing' and is designed to be attractive, for the sake of producing fruit which proprogates more plants, that is, more people.

Why does the glory of God seem to be so exceptional that the silent majority of Christians don't have any expectations about it? Because faith is not working in that dimension or aspect of God's kingdom. The picture is like the man with a withered hand, who Jesus healed. What did Jesus require of the man? He first said 'stand up in front of the church", then "stretch out your hand!". Jesus always required the seeker who wants healing to do some work to prove they believe He is the Christ. That's not mean spirited but purely a neccessity since acting and speaking in faith is the catalyst to release God's power. Jesus deliberately did his major healing works on the Sabbath. Consider that the Pharisees persecuted Him for doing this. It appears that Jesus pursued that confrontation because it exposed the error of the rabbinic tradition and their evil theology. What is amazing is the way they accused Jesus of working on the Sabbath, eventhough He was not doing any literal work, apart from laying hands on the sick person. They didn't deny that genuine miracles were happening, they just denied that God approved healing on the Sabbath. Think about the madness, the insanity of that mindset. The Lord God commands Israel to cease from work on the Sabbath. This rule is designed to promote submission to God-dependency instead of self-dependency. The Lord rested on the seventh day of creation. In their presumptuous view, the Pharisees must reckon God was unwilling to do any work at all, for any reason whatever, every Sunday according to an Earth calendar. The Lord God of Israel can't work in the eternal realm, if that work happens to be scheduled on the Sabbath according to the spacetime dimension of Earth. Life on earth is imposing a limitation on life in Heaven. The Sabbath is actually the day when God is seeking to show his glory by doing miraculous work, which makes a mans natural work very small by comparison. The 'Day' of rest is eternal and refers to the Light of life which the Lord God gives to heal, restore or create life.
